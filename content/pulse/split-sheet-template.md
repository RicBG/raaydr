# Split Sheet Template: A Free Music Split Sheet You Can Copy

**The Pulse · The RAAYDR Blog**
*By Ric, Founder of RAAYDR · October 2026*

A split sheet is one page that says who wrote a song and who gets what share. You fill it in while everyone is still in the room. It takes ten minutes and it saves months of arguing later.

Copy the template below. Then read the section on mistakes, because that is where the money gets stuck.

## What is a split sheet?

It is a signed record of who owns what in a song. Each person who helped write it is listed, with the share they own. The shares add up to 100%.

It matters because money cannot be paid out cleanly without it. If two people register the same song with different shares, the societies and platforms can hold the payment until it is sorted.

## How to fill it in

1. **Use legal names.** Stage names are fine as well, but the society will want the name on your membership.
2. **Add your IPI number.** If you are a PRS member this is on your account. If you are not a member yet, leave it blank and add it when you join.
3. **Make the shares add up to 100%.** Check it twice.
4. **Sign it the same day.** Memory gets worse and sessions get busy. Do it before anyone leaves.
5. **Everyone keeps a copy.** A photo of the signed page counts.

## Writing split or master split

There are two things in a song, and each has its own split.

- **The writing.** The words and the melody. Writers and sometimes producers share this.
- **The recording.** The master. This usually belongs to the artist or whoever paid for the session.

A split sheet normally covers the writing. If the producer is also taking a share of the recording, write that down as well. Say which split you mean.

## The mistakes that hold up payments

- **Shares that do not add up.** 50, 30 and 30 is 110.
- **Different numbers on different registrations.** Each person registers the song. The numbers have to match.
- **Nothing signed.** A verbal yes is a memory, not a record.
- **No date.** If you do not know when it was written, you cannot say which agreement applied.
- **Samples left out.** If you used someone else's music, that has its own permission.

## FAQ

**Is a split sheet legally binding?**
A signed and dated split sheet is strong evidence of what everyone agreed. If a lot of money is involved, ask a music lawyer to look at it. This page is not legal advice.

**When should I fill in a split sheet?**
As soon as the song is written, before anyone leaves the session.

**Do producers go on a split sheet?**
If a producer helped write the song, such as the melody or arrangement, they can be listed as a writer. If they only produced the recording, their share is usually agreed separately.

**Do I need a split sheet if I wrote it alone?**
No. It still helps to date and keep a record of what you wrote.

**What if we cannot agree the split?**
Do not register the song until you do. Registering conflicting shares is what causes the delays.
