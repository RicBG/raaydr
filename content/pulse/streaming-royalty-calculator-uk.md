## The sum to do yourself

Streams on a platform, times the rate for that platform. Add the platforms together. Take off your distributor's cut. That is your estimate.

Three things move the answer:

- **Where the listener lives.** A stream from a UK paid listener earns more than a stream from a free tier listener somewhere else.
- **Free or paid.** Ad supported plays earn less than subscriber plays.
- **The month.** Platforms share a pot of money. The pot is shared out by all streams, so your rate shifts every month.

On Spotify there is another catch. A track with fewer than 1,000 streams in the last twelve months earns nothing at all. We explain it in [the 1,000 stream rule](/pulse/spotify-1000-stream-rule).

## FAQ

**How much does Spotify pay per stream in the UK?**
There is no official rate. A commonly measured figure is about {{spotify.perStream}} a stream, before your distributor takes its fee.

**How do I calculate my streaming royalties?**
Multiply your streams on each platform by that platform's rate, add them up, then take off your distributor's fee. The result is an estimate, not a promise.

**Is Apple Music better than Spotify for artists?**
Per stream, Apple Music is usually estimated to pay more. It has a smaller audience, so total earnings depend on where your listeners are.

**Why do calculators give different answers?**
Platforms do not publish rates, so each calculator uses its own estimate. They also differ on currency, listener country and plan type.

**Does this include songwriting royalties?**
No. Publishing royalties are collected separately through a society such as PRS.
