# Help

*If something is not working, or you cannot find what you need, write to us.*

## Contact us

Email **help@raaydr.com**. Tell us the email address on your account and what happened, and we will start from there.

## Signing in

If you have forgotten your password, use "Forgot your password?" on the sign in page and we will send you a link. If the email does not arrive, check your junk folder, wait a few minutes, then write to us.

## Your Day One price

If you joined the waitlist with a different email address, write to help@raaydr.com and we will honour your Day One price.

## Your data and your account

You can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data at any time. How to ask, and what happens next, is in our [Privacy policy](/privacy).

## Artists, producers and songwriters

Payments to artists, producers and songwriters start when paid listening opens. Nothing has been paid yet, and nothing on RAAYDR is money that is waiting for you. When payments start, every figure will say where it came from.

If you are credited on a track and cannot find it, or a credit is wrong, write to us and tell us the track and your name as it appears on it.
