## 5. How creators get paid

**Each listener's money goes to the artists that listener actually listens to**, in proportion to their counted listening time that month. Nobody's subscription is pooled with anybody else's.

**What gets shared.** From each listener's monthly payment we first take out VAT, the songwriting royalties we pay to PRS for Music, and card and payout fees. What's left is shared like this:

| Who | Share of what's left |
| --- | --- |
| Artists, by that listener's attention | 55% |
| Tastemakers | up to 10%. Anything tastemakers don't earn goes to artists |
| RAAYDR | 35% |

Referral money (section 7) comes out of RAAYDR's share, never yours. We publish these figures on raaydr.com and give you at least 30 days' notice before any of them changes.

**What counts.** A play counts once it passes our listening threshold. Your own plays of your own music, tastemakers' plays, and our test accounts' plays never count. If a listener plays nothing in a month, RAAYDR never keeps the artists' share of their money. If they have listened before, it follows what they played in their last month of listening. If they are new and haven't played anything yet, it waits and follows their first listening. If they leave without ever playing anything, it is shared across all artists in proportion to everyone's listening that month.

**Your money, step by step.**

1. **In progress:** a listener's month is still running. What you see is an estimate.
2. **Confirmed:** their month has closed. The amount is fixed and recorded to you.
3. **Payable:** confirmed, you have set up payouts, and everything you're owed adds up to £25 or more.
4. **Paid:** sent to your bank.

**When.** We pay once a month, on the 10th. A listener's month is confirmed once it closes, and paid on the next payout day after that, so your first payment comes in the second payout after you start earning.

**The £25 minimum** counts everything you're owed from every source together: your own tracks, your splits on other people's tracks, referrals and tastemaker money. Under £25, it rolls over to the next month and is shown as owed.

**Setting up payouts.** We pay through Stripe. You'll need to verify who you are and add your bank details with Stripe, and agree to Stripe's terms. Until you do, your money is held and shown as owed. It is never lost and never given to anyone else.

**Refunds and chargebacks.** If a listener's payment is refunded or reversed after you've been paid for it, we take that amount off your next payment and show it on your statement. We never take money from your bank.

**Fake listening.** If we find plays were faked or bought, they don't count, we can hold payment while we check, and we can take back money already paid for them.

**Statements.** You get a monthly statement showing every source, every deduction and every payment, which you can download.

**Tax.** You are responsible for your own tax. We may ask for details we need by law.

## 6. Splits for producers, songwriters and collaborators

If you upload a track, you can split what it earns with the people who made it: producers, songwriters, featured artists, mixers and others.

- You set each person's share. The shares on a track always add up to 100%.
- Each person is invited and must accept their split. Once accepted, their share is paid to them directly every month, the same way as section 5.
- A change to a split applies from the date it is made, never backwards. Money already confirmed stays with whoever it was confirmed to.
- Until an invited person accepts, the person who uploaded the track keeps that share. The invite lasts 90 days. Once they accept, their share is paid to them from then on.
- If collaborators disagree about a split, we pay what is recorded until you agree a change. We can hold the disputed share while you sort it out.
- Splits on RAAYDR cover money RAAYDR pays. They don't replace any agreement you have with each other outside RAAYDR.
