# Privacy policy

*Last updated 3 October 2026.*

**Who's responsible.** RAAYDR LIMITED (company 17418893, 66 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NA) decides how your personal data is used. Contact us about it at hello@raaydr.com.

**What we collect, and why**

| What | Why | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your email, name, handle and the roles you choose | To run your account | Our contract with you |
| Your profile, uploads, credits and splits | To show your music and pay the right people | Our contract with you |
| What you listen to, when and for how long | To pay artists by attention, show you your listening, and stop fake plays | Our contract with you |
| Payment details | Taken and held by Stripe, not us. We see your plan, payment status and the last four digits of your card | Our contract with you |
| Identity and bank details for payouts | Collected and held by Stripe to pay you | Our contract with you, and the law |
| Sign-up details from raaydr.com: email, role, artist name and genre, how you reached us, and the country you signed up from (worked out from your connection; we don't keep your IP address) | To get you set up, and learn which of our channels work | Our legitimate interests |
| Cookies for analytics and ads (Google Analytics, Meta) | To measure our marketing | Your consent, which you can withdraw any time |
| Messages you send us | To reply and support you | Our legitimate interests |
| Payment and payout records | Tax and accounting | The law |

**Who sees it.** Artists see how their music is played. They only see your name among their top listeners if you switch on "show my name" in your settings; otherwise you appear without a name. If you follow an artist, they can see your handle and photo. Other people only see your name where you choose to show it, for example on your profile or a public playlist. We never sell your data.

**Who helps us run RAAYDR.** Supabase (our databases, in London), Vercel (website and app hosting, and cookieless visit counts), Cloudflare (storing music, artwork and profile images), GitHub (where our code runs its scheduled jobs), Railway (processing uploads), Stripe (payments and payouts), Resend (emails), Google (email and analytics) and Meta (ad measurement). Some of them handle data outside the UK; where they do, they use the safeguards UK law requires.

**How long we keep it.** Your account data while your account is open. Payment and payout records for six years, because the law requires it. Listening records as long as we need them to calculate and evidence payments. Sign-up details from raaydr.com until you have an account or ask us to remove them.

**Your rights.** You can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. Email hello@raaydr.com and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

**Age.** RAAYDR is for people aged 18 and over.

**Changes.** We'll tell you about important changes before they apply.
