# Cookie policy

*Last updated 3 October 2026.*

We use a few cookies that the site needs to work, like keeping you signed in. With your permission we also use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site, and Meta's pixel to measure our ads. Nothing beyond the essentials runs until you agree in the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, we treat that as a no and don't ask. We also count visits and measure page speed through Vercel, which sets no cookies and can't identify you.
